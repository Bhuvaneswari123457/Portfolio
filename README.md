Sky Quest Portfolio

A game-style personal portfolio for Chodisetty Bhuvaneswari, Software Engineer (B.Tech CSE, NIT Rourkela '25). Scroll through the page like a quest: earn XP, unlock sections, collect skills and explore experience as levels.

Live site: https://bhuvaneswari123457.github.io/Portfolio/

Features
XP bar and flying plane at the top that follows your scroll progress, with XP and level-ups as sections unlock
Quest log: work experience shown as Exp 1 to Exp 4 (Barclays, Gap Inc., FTP and Outlier, Snorkel for Amazon)
Skill tree: tap each skill to collect it and earn XP
Education and Subjects: B.Tech, Intermediate and Class 10, plus core subjects as unlockable cards
Projects: FTP logistics bots, Smart Research Assistant and ECG Signal Classification, each with a details pop-up and links
Certifications as achievement badges
Download resume button
Light and dark theme toggle, and a sound on/off button
Responsive layout that works on phones and desktops
